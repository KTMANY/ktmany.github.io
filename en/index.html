Cyber security & consulting

Security driven by risk – not by buzzwords

Small and medium-sized businesses are popular targets. We help you close the most important gaps first – clearly explained and with a concrete plan.

BSI IT-Grundschutz

IT-Grundschutz by the German Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) is the established standard for information security in Germany. We support you from the first inventory to a robust security concept.

  • Structure analysis & protection requirements
  • Modelling according to the IT-Grundschutz Compendium
  • IT-Grundschutz check (target/actual comparison)
  • Basic, standard or core protection
  • Risk analysis according to BSI Standard 200-3
  • Preparation for ISO 27001 based on IT-Grundschutz

Security check

An inventory of your IT, cloud services and applications. Result: a prioritised list of risks and concrete measures.

NIS2 & ISO 27001

We assess whether NIS2 applies to you and support you in building an information security management system (ISMS).

Security by design

Security belongs in the architecture, not at the end. We support teams with threat modelling, secure code and reviews.

Cloud & access

Secure configuration of cloud environments, Microsoft 365 and identities – with multi-factor authentication and least privilege.

Business continuity

Backup strategy, incident response plan and exercises – so everyone knows what to do and operations recover quickly.

Awareness

Practical training on phishing, passwords and secure work habits – because most attacks start with people.
